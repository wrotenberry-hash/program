# Art brief: Program

For a contract illustrator. Send as-is.

## The game

Program is a mobile web game for die-hard college football fans. You run
your school's football program: build facilities, hire coaches, pick a game
plan, and play a weekly League against rival schools' fans. Fans of the same
school band together in a faction, and the season builds to Rivalry Week.

It plays in a phone browser, portrait, one hand. Sessions are short: collect,
upgrade, claim, check the result.

## The look

Colorful, chunky, cartoon mobile strategy game. The reference is **Last War**
and games like it: a bright base you tap around, rounded 3D-ish buildings,
glowing buttons, gold coins, a big orange power number. Inviting and
playful, never gritty, never a spreadsheet.

The interface already exists with a set palette (deep navy in dark mode, soft
blue-white in light mode, gold, green, orange, purple). Screenshots are
attached. Your art sits on top of that, in **both light and dark mode**.

## Hard rules

These are legal requirements, not taste.

- **All original.** No real school logos, mascots, wordmarks, fight songs,
  stadium names, or trademarked trade dress.
- **No school colors as identity.** Do not make "the orange one" read as a
  specific real school. Factions get a generic crest system (below) that the
  game recolors.
- **No real people.** Coaches are fictional. No likeness of real coaches,
  players, or public figures.
- **No real-athlete likeness**, including jersey numbers paired with
  recognizable features.

## Deliverables

### 1. Campus buildings (the home screen) — highest priority

Six buildings, each with **three visual tiers** (levels 1–2, 3–4, 5) so
upgrades feel visible. Isometric, same angle and scale, transparent
background. Plus one "under construction" overlay (scaffolding, crane) that
fits on any building.

| Building | What it is |
|---|---|
| Booster Club | Donors' clubhouse. Makes money. Should feel like a coin source. |
| Stadium | The centerpiece. Biggest building. |
| Weight Room | Strength and conditioning. |
| Practice Facility | Indoor field and turf. |
| Film Room | Scheme install, screens, playbooks. |
| Academic Center | Classrooms, study hall. |

Also: a campus ground tile or backdrop the buildings sit on (grass, paths,
a scoreboard), in a day version and a night version.

### 2. Faction crest system

A shield or crest built in layers (shape, inner emblem, banner) that the game
can recolor and combine. About 8 shapes and 16 emblems (animals, objects,
abstract marks; none that suggest a real mascot). Delivered as SVG with
separate color layers.

### 3. Coach portraits

Eleven fictional coaches, head-and-shoulders, same framing. Three rarity
frames: common, rare, epic. Diverse in age, gender, and background. Their
names and roles are in the game; send the list on request.

### 4. Reward and currency art

Coin (single and pile), budget chest or bag, power bolt, scouting report,
trophy (weekly rivalry trophy, League champion trophy, season prize), daily
check-in streak badge.

### 5. Game day

A small set of key-moment vignettes for results: touchdown run, deep pass,
interception, goal-line stand, field goal, sack. Generic players in
non-real uniforms that the game can tint.

### 6. App icon and share card background

App icon at standard sizes. A share-card background for "we beat our rival."

## Format

- SVG where possible. Otherwise PNG at 3x, transparent background.
- Readable at small sizes on a phone (buildings about 120–160 px wide on
  screen).
- Source files included. Full rights assigned to the company.

## Priority if budget or time is short

1 (campus buildings), then 4 (rewards), then 2 (crests), then 3 (coaches),
then 5 and 6.

## Timing

Campus buildings and reward art by **Nov 6, 2026**. The rest can follow.
